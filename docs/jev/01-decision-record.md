# Decision record

**Last updated:** 2026-09-24  
**Status:** Initial discovery complete; implementation and JEV results pending.

## Target

Define a Mexico-first website message and funnel that attracts qualified organizations seeking to improve IT/product capability, with AI as an intervention rather than the automatic starting point.

## Buyer hypothesis

- **Primary A:** Mexico-based SME owners/operators.
- **Primary B:** Mexico-based mid-market or enterprise transformation leaders.
- **Conditional C:** Bilingual technical leaders in the US/Mexico cross-border market, only if evidence supports it.
- A and B share a problem space but may have different proof and buying language.

## Problem hypothesis

Organizations want to improve IT services and product capabilities, but often move toward AI adoption before knowing where investment will create the most value.

## Offer hypothesis

- **Primary:** fixed-scope paid Clarity Sprint.
- **Expansion:** open-ended consulting or advisory after the Sprint.
- The Sprint is a hypothesis until qualified conversations and paid engagements validate its scope.

## Message hypothesis

```text
Problem: You want to improve IT and product capability.
Tension: AI is one possible intervention, not the starting point.
Promise: Identify the highest-value improvements before building or buying the wrong solution.
Offer: A prioritized, evidence-backed roadmap through the Clarity Sprint.
```

## Initial funnel

```text
site visit
→ CTA click
→ Calendly booking
→ qualified conversation
→ paid Clarity Sprint
```

- **Primary initial conversion:** completed Calendly booking.
- **Quality metric:** qualified conversation.
- **Revenue metric:** paid Clarity Sprint.
- No historical data exists yet.

## Decisions

| Decision | Status | Confidence | Evidence or gate |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Clarity Sprint is the provisional primary offer | Hypothesis | Medium | Matches the stated problem and offers a defined first purchase; validate willingness to pay |
| Keep one shared core before market splitting | Confirmed for current phase | High | No evidence yet that Mexico and US require different funnels |
| Use language as the first market proxy | Confirmed for baseline only | High | Observable immediately, but not proof of geography |
| Track booking and qualified conversation separately | Confirmed for measurement design | Medium | Booking volume alone cannot show lead quality |
| Defer conventional A/B testing | Confirmed for current phase | High | Fewer than 100 estimated sessions/month; low-frequency conversion |
| Instrument before strategic message changes | Confirmed for current phase | High | No first-party event layer or historical baseline exists |
| Split Mexico and US messages | Deferred | High | Revisit only after qualified-conversation and market evidence |

Two independent reviews evaluated the same eight frozen questions. Both agreed on all eight provisional decisions. One required stricter qualification and baseline gates; those gates are now part of this record.

## Open questions

- What is the exact first problem buyers recognize in their own words?
- Which buyer role and company size produce the strongest qualified conversations?
- What proof makes a paid Clarity Sprint feel low-risk?
- Does the Sprint need a fixed scope and price before conversion improves?
- Are English and Spanish visitors different in problem, source, or intent, or only in language?

## Do not assume yet

- That AI is the primary buying trigger.
- That Spanish means Mexico or English means the US.
- That a booking is a qualified opportunity.
- That more traffic is the immediate bottleneck.
- That a separate locale funnel is justified.

**Next review:** after the first five qualified conversations or the first 30 days of instrumented traffic, whichever comes first.
